VOCABULARY
きょうしつ (kyoushitsu) - classroom
となり (tonari) - next to / beside
すき (suki) - to like
よろしく (yoroshiku) - please treat me well

GRAMMAR
Noun + が すき - Use this to say you like something
Place + に すわる - Use this to say where you sit
